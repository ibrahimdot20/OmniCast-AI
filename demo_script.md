# 🎬 OmniCast AI: 4-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

This script is structured specifically for recording the **4-Minute Demo Video** required by Google's **All Things Agentic Hackathon**.

---

### [0:00 - 0:45] The Problem & Introduction
* **Visual:** Screen shows the creator opening 6 browser tabs (ChatGPT, Midjourney, ElevenLabs, Notion, LinkedIn, Twitter).
* **Narrator:** 
  > *"Every creator, founder, and marketer knows the daily agony of content production. Turning one idea or product launch into a multi-platform campaign takes 4 to 6 hours of repetitive copy-pasting, formatting for different social rules, generating images, and drafting video scripts. Standard chatbots only give you generic walls of text. Today, we built **OmniCast AI: The Autonomous Multi-Platform Studio Agent** powered by the **Antigravity SDK**, **Gemini 3.7 Flash**, and deployed on **Google Cloud Run**."*

---

### [0:45 - 1:30] One-Shot Universal Input & Autonomous Deep Research
* **Visual:** User enters one prompt into OmniCast AI: *"Autonomous AI Agents are replacing simple chatbots in 2026"* and clicks **"Forge Campaign"**.
* **Narrator:**
  > *"OmniCast AI requires zero rigid forms. You enter a raw idea, custom rules, or paste an article URL. In one click, the Antigravity Swarm activates. 
  > Notice what happens first: Stage 1 is not copying text—it is our **Autonomous Deep Research Agent**. It crawls facts, analyzes real audience sentiment, identifies viral angles, and generates a structured Research Dossier right before our eyes."*

---

### [1:30 - 2:30] Sequential Platform Generation & Tool Execution
* **Visual:** Watch the live Agent Radar pulsate as cards pop in sequentially: Strategic Plan $\rightarrow$ LinkedIn $\rightarrow$ Twitter $\rightarrow$ WhatsApp $\rightarrow$ Newsletter $\rightarrow$ Facebook $\rightarrow$ Instagram $\rightarrow$ Media.
* **Narrator:**
  > *"Next, the Strategic Planner architects a cross-platform narrative, and dispatches specialized sub-agents. 
  > Each platform appears sequentially with native formatting:
  > - **LinkedIn** gets executive storytelling and spacing.
  > - **Twitter/X** gets a 280-character viral hook and thread.
  > - **WhatsApp** is formatted with native bold tags, emojis, and bullet points.
  > - **Email Newsletter** is formatted with 3 subject lines and H2 headers.
  > Then, our deterministic multimodal tools kick in: **Google Veo Video Scene Director** engineers 3D camera prompts, **Google Imagen** renders high-res 16:9 and 9:16 thumbnails, and **Google Cloud TTS** synthesizes a studio audio voiceover that we can play right here."*

---

### [2:30 - 3:15] Interactive Card Controls & Isolated Regeneration
* **Visual:** Narrator clicks **Copy**, then clicks **Edit** to modify text inline, and then clicks **Regenerate** on the WhatsApp card with a custom tweak.
* **Narrator:**
  > *"Every card is fully interactive. You can copy in one click, download individual posts, or edit text directly in place. 
  > Even better: if you want to tweak just one post—say, make the WhatsApp message more urgent—you click **Regenerate**. An isolated sub-agent rewrites only that specific card without resetting or touching the rest of your campaign!"*

---

### [3:15 - 4:00] 1-Click ZIP Export & Cloud Architecture Wrap-up
* **Visual:** Narrator clicks **"Download All (.ZIP)"**, opens the downloaded archive showing organized folders with Markdown, `.txt`, `.png` images, and `.mp3` audio. Then shows the Cloud Run console / architecture slide.
* **Narrator:**
  > *"With one final click, we download the entire production bundle as a ZIP file—complete with scripts, images, and audio files ready to publish. 
  > Built with the **Antigravity Python SDK**, **Gemini 3.7 Flash**, and containerized for **Google Cloud Run**, OmniCast AI turns a 5-hour multi-tool headache into a 30-second autonomous studio. Thank you!"*
